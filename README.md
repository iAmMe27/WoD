## Hardware Requirements

**We do not post minimum requirements to run the list.**

Why?  
Because it depends.  
Some people are perfectly content with 40-60 fps, others find the game unplayable under 80.  

The list should start on anything halfway modern with 6GB VRAM.  
Every system is different, so we can't tell you what kind of performance you can expect with a i9 36-9000 and a RTX 9070 Ti Superduper either.

Whether the list runs good enough for you is for you to decide.  
And if it doesn't, you'll have to figure out your own hardware and look for the performance yourself.  
That's just one of the responsibilities of owning your own PC.

> [!NOTE]
> If you're running the list on a card that can't do DLSS (e.g. GTX 1xxx series), you will have to disable 'Custom Upscaler' in MO2 for the list to start.

> If you have the Game of the Year (GOTY) edition of Fallout 4, you're good to go, it includes all the needed DLC.
>

## What we do not support


### External SSDs and HDDs
They are just too slow for the list to work, and if it has worked before or has worked for other lists great, but you're not gonna do yourself or us a favor by using one.  
Not even if they are very fast Thunderbolt 16, 480Tbps. Sorry.

You will get random performance drops and almost impossible to figure our crashes, therefore we can't support it.


### Linux
All members of the dev team use Windows to run their modlists.  
So we can't help you with a modlinst install on Linux.

> [!NOTE]
> That does not necessarily mean that the modlist can't be run on Linux.  
> It just means we can't help you to set it up.
>
> Feel free to check out the #wod-modified channel on Discord, search in there and ask around.  
> As Linux becomes more relevant for gaming, chances are someone already did it.


### Controller
All members of the dev team use keyboard and mouse to play the list.  
So we don't have any experience setting up controller, and we do not make keybinds for it.  

> [!NOTE]
> Chances are someone is playing the list with a controller.  
> Feel free to search and ask in #wod-modified on discord.

### Male player character
This modlist is created entirely with a female protagonist in mind.  

> [!NOTE]
> It is possible to play as a male, it's just not recommended.  
> We don't playtest male PC at all.
> You will miss out on content as most NSFW mods are written with a female PC in mind.  
> 
> If you want to play as a male, apply the appropriate [Optional MCM Profile](https://github.com/iAmMe27/WoD/blob/main/ModSetup.md#i-am-a-male-character).
